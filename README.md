# gold-snap
[Wow Addon] Battle text for your loot!

Make every bit of gold feel good.

When you gain gold, the amount pops up like battle text right where you clicked. Painted coins then fly across the screen to your gold counter, and the digits roll up to your new total while the text swells and settles. It works for corpses, quest rewards and mail, and when you loot a group of mobs at once you get one combined total instead of a pile of overlapping numbers.

## Features
- **Cursor popup:** the amount appears at your cursor, battle-text style, and combines multiple corpses into one total.
- **Flying coins:** painted gold, silver and copper coins travel from the popup to your counter.
- **Rolling counter:** the digits count up over a configurable length (3 seconds by default).
- **Move and resize anywhere:** drag the counter wherever you like. While unlocked, drag the corner grip to make it as big or as small as you want. There is no padding around the text, so it can sit right at the edge of the screen.
- **Gold stats:** click the counter to see gold per hour, gold this session, all-time gold on this character, and a breakdown by source (quests, looted, mail, other).
- **Fully configurable:** choose your font (built-in fonts plus anything from LibSharedMedia), set the popup and counter sizes, change the roll length, switch between the painted coins and the game's own coin icons, and lock the counter in place.

## How to use
- **Left-click** the counter for stats
- **Right-click** the counter for options
- **Drag** the counter to move it, **drag the corner** to resize (when unlocked)
- `/gsnap` opens options, `/gsnap test` plays a test drop, `/gsnap group` plays a test group loot

## Supported versions
Catalclysm Classic

## Notes
- All-time stats count from the day you install the addon. Session stats reset when you reload.
- Gold from vendors, the auction house and trades is animated too and shows up as "Other" in the stats. You can switch this off in options.
